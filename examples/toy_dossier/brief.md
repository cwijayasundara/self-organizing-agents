# Launch price for a B2B note-taking app in Germany

Recommend a launch price (EUR per seat per month) for a hypothetical B2B note-taking app in Germany.
Use only the facts in this brief. Do not use outside knowledge or search for other data.

## Facts

1. Competitor Notiva charges 8.00 EUR per seat per month.
2. Competitor Memoro charges 12.50 EUR per seat per month.
3. Competitor Jotwerk charges 15.00 EUR per seat per month.
4. Our hosting and support cost is 2.40 EUR per seat per month.
5. Our payment processing fee is 3% of revenue.
6. Our sales and marketing cost is 1.60 EUR per seat per month.
7. The target gross margin is 60% of the price, after hosting, support, payment fees and sales and marketing cost.
8. German customers require EU data residency; our hosting cost already includes it.
9. The first sales target is 40 teams averaging 25 seats each.
10. Prices are quoted excluding VAT.
